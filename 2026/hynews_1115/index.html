Tea Hot Filling Line

Published 2026-11-15 · KIWL Machine

The difference between a workable tea hot filling line and an expensive mistake usually shows up six months later, in downtime and in service calls. This guide covers the specification points that decide the outcome for mid-size beverage producers, in the order they are best resolved.

What This Equipment Does

Every machine on a line should have one sentence that explains why it exists. For this tea hot filling line it is: it processes 1.5 L product at 2065–3065 units per hour, consistently, without the mid-size beverage producers team chasing it around.

Key Technical Specifications

Read a tea hot filling line specification for what is measurable: rate 2065–3065 units per hour, deviation ±0.3 ml, container 1.5 L, control 4-inch HMI, load 8.5–11.5 kW, footprint 1600 × 2200 × 3200 mm, changeover 10 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

The working width of 1600 mm on this tea hot filling line determines what upstream equipment can feed it, so confirm it against the actual conveyor pitch rather than the catalogue.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the tea hot filling line on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 1.5 L product with a 10-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

The tea hot filling line was designed so the routine work happens during a changeover rather than during production. Seals, sensors and drives are grouped for access, which is what allows a 2065 unit per hour mid-size beverage producers production line to maintain without a shutdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the tea hot filling line run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 1.5 L product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

What is the actual connected load of the tea hot filling line?

The reference build draws 8.5–11.5 kW. Confirm the starting current and the supply arrangement at the site before ordering.

Does the tea hot filling line need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 2200 × 3200 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
