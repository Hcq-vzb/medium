Date Printer for Bottles

Published 2026-11-19 · KIWL Machine

A date printer for bottles sits on the critical path of the mid-size beverage producers production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a date printer for bottles.

What This Equipment Does

A date printer for bottles earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 6285–7785 units per hour for 330 ml can product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask the supplier to state the substrate the rate applies to. A date printer for bottles quoting 6285–7785 impressions per minute on 330 ml can format has to say whether that is PE, PP or paper, because the ink and the drying differ.

Integration With Your Line

The print width of 2400 mm has to match the actual cup body the mid-size beverage producers operation buys, which is worth confirming in writing with the paper supplier.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the date printer for bottles over five years rather than one budget cycle. Plates, ink supply and the 0.5 ml registration tolerance all have different cost curves over that horizon.

Maintenance and Service

Choose a supplier that holds spare parts locally and answers support requests in writing. Preventive work on the date printer for bottles concentrates on the seals, sensors and drives — wear items rather than failure items.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this date printer for bottles?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 330 ml can format takes longer.

What is the actual connected load of the date printer for bottles?

The reference build draws 1.5–2.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

Does the date printer for bottles need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 2400 × 2800 × 2100 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
