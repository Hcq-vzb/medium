Bottle Blowing Machine

Two bottle blowing machine quotations can quote the same rate and still describe different machines. This guide covers the mid-size beverage producers details that tell them apart — accuracy, materials, control and changeover — and what to write into the order.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of a bottle blowing machine is not its quoted maximum but its behaviour at 3902 units per hour when product density shifts and viscosity climbs — the situation the mid-size beverage producers production line meets every day.

Key Technical Specifications

A blow moulding quotation should be reduced to five numbers: rate 3902–5402 bottles per hour, volume 3–4 L, tolerance 3.0 %, load 1.5–2.5 kW and footprint 1200 × 3600 × 2800 mm. This bottle blowing machine is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

A bottle blowing machine becomes part of the mid-size beverage producers production line when the same recipe reaches it, the same cleaning reaches it and the same fault code reaches the operator. This build is specified around those three handovers.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest case for a bottle blowing machine is the downtime it prevents during a 10-minute changeover. At 3902 units per hour, the mid-size beverage producers production line recovers exactly what the stop takes away.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive discipline on a bottle blowing machine is easier when the intervals are stated in cycles rather than months. At 3902–5402 units per hour, this build's seals and sensors reach end of life far sooner than a quarterly check would suggest.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this bottle blowing machine?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 330 ml can format takes longer.

What does the bottle blowing machine do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

Does the bottle blowing machine need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1200 × 3600 × 2800 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
