Conveyor System

Published 2026-10-26 · KIWL Machine

Everything that follows concerns a single machine: a conveyor system for mid-size beverage producers. The rate, the materials, the control system, the changeover and the acceptance checks — presented so that a technical buyer and an operations manager can use the same document.

What This Equipment Does

A conveyor system earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 7839–10839 units per hour for 16 oz product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that gets missed is the changeover. A conveyor system with a 10-minute changeover across 16 oz format and 304 stainless steel contact parts is a different proposition from one without, even at the same 7839–10839 units per hour.

Integration With Your Line

The costliest mistake is treating a conveyor system as a standalone machine. Once it accepts a line encoder signal and works across a 1800 mm working width, it becomes part of the mid-size beverage producers control loop and recipe changes stop being manual.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the conveyor system to real 16 oz volume, then let the 3.0 ml tolerance and the 7839–10839 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive discipline on a conveyor system is easier when the intervals are stated in cycles rather than months. At 7839–10839 units per hour, this build's seals and sensors reach end of life far sooner than a quarterly check would suggest.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the conveyor system behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the conveyor system determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

What is the actual connected load of the conveyor system?

The reference build draws 3–4 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

Can the conveyor system handle a 16 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 10-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 4-inch HMI.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
