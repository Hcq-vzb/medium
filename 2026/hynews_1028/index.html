Palletizer

Published 2026-10-28 · KIWL Machine

The palletizer is easier to specify than most mid-size beverage producers buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

Every machine on a line should have one sentence that explains why it exists. For this palletizer it is: it processes 2 L product at 1279–1929 units per hour, consistently, without the mid-size beverage producers team chasing it around.

Key Technical Specifications

A palletizer is easy to compare when four numbers are isolated from the option list. Here they are: ±1.5 ml, 1279–1929 units per hour, 21–28 kW, and 3200 × 2200 × 2400 mm, on 2 L product.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The palletizer is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the mid-size beverage producers workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The business case for a palletizer is arithmetic before it is technical: 1.5 ml per unit, 1279–1929 units per hour, and a shift count that adds up across a year. Work it that way and the answer is usually within 12–36 months at regional mid-size beverage producers scale.

Maintenance and Service

Availability planning for a palletizer starts with the assumption that a seal will need replacing in the next quarter. Holding the SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact-part spares on site is what keeps a 8-minute repair a 8-minute repair.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the palletizer handle a 2 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 8-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

What is the delivery lead time for the palletizer?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 2 L set is included.

Does the palletizer need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 2200 × 2400 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
