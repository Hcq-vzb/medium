PE Film Shrink Packer

Published 2026-11-20 · KIWL Machine

Choosing the right PE film shrink packer is a practical decision for mid-size beverage producers. This guide covers the criteria that matter when specifying, integrating and accepting a PE film shrink packer on a production line, with the figures a buyer should confirm in writing before placing an order.

What This Equipment Does

A PE film shrink packer handles a defined step in the production line for mid-size beverage producers. This build runs at 5530–7030 units per hour for {sz} product, and the configuration directly affects output consistency, labour and product quality.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, material contact parts (typically 304/316L stainless), control type (PLC vs relay), utilities demand and changeover time. The reference build for this PE film shrink packer is 304 stainless steel with a 7-inch colour touch, a 12–17 kW connected load and a {fw_a} × {fw_b} × 3200 mm footprint, and these figures set both the price and the long-term flexibility of the line.

Integration With Your Line

Plan upstream buffer, downstream transfer and shared CIP or control logic so the PE film shrink packer does not become the bottleneck. Compatibility with existing tanks and conveyors avoids costly rework, and this build accepts a standard PLC interface for the upstream machine.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Size the PE film shrink packer to peak demand with modest headroom, then model payback from labour savings, yield gain and reduced downtime. At 5530–7030 units per hour a typical mid-size beverage producers operation recovers the investment within 12–36 months at regional scale.

Maintenance and Service

Choose suppliers offering spare parts stock, remote support and clear manuals. Preventive maintenance on seals, sensors and drives keeps availability high, and on this PE film shrink packer the wear items are the seals, the drive and the guide rails.

  • Check the guarding and the emergency pull-cord system across the section

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the conveyor match an existing line height?

Yes. The frame height is adjustable, and the exact working height is confirmed against your line drawing at quotation.

Is it suitable for washdown areas?

Yes. A stainless frame and food-grade chain version is available for hygienic environments.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
