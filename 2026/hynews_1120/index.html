PE Film Shrink Packer

Published 2026-11-20 · KIWL Machine

This guide is written for the person who signs the order and the person who will run the machine. Both need the same mid-size beverage producers figures, presented so that neither has to translate them first.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a PE film shrink packer is not the demonstration but the third month. At 5530–7030 units per hour for 2 L product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

A PE film shrink packer is easy to compare when four numbers are isolated from the option list. Here they are: ±0.3 ml, 5530–7030 units per hour, 12–17 kW, and 1800 × 2200 × 3200 mm, on 2 L product.

Integration With Your Line

A PE film shrink packer becomes part of the mid-size beverage producers production line when the same recipe reaches it, the same cleaning reaches it and the same fault code reaches the operator. This build is specified around those three handovers.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the PE film shrink packer over five years rather than one budget cycle. 304 stainless steel contact parts, a 0.3 ml tolerance and a 15-minute changeover have different cost curves, and the purchase price tells you very little about any of them.

Maintenance and Service

Treat the PE film shrink packer service records as production data. Once a few months of interventions are logged, the failure pattern on the seals and the drive usually tells you where the real risk sits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this PE film shrink packer hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.3 ml on 2 L product at 5530–7030 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How is the PE film shrink packer documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

What is the delivery lead time for the PE film shrink packer?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 2 L set is included.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
