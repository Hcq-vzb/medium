Juice Blending And Pasteurizing Unit

Published 2026-11-05 · KIWL Machine

Before any juice blending and pasteurizing unit quotation is compared, the rate has to be tied to a product and a format. This guide does that for mid-size beverage producers operations and then works through the rest of the decision.

What This Equipment Does

The unit a juice blending and pasteurizing unit performs is easy to describe and hard to do well: it takes 500 ml can product and delivers 2159–3159 units per hour, and everything downstream is calibrated against that figure.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a juice blending and pasteurizing unit on the specification that limits your output: the 3.0 ml tolerance, the 316L stainless steel contact parts and the 15-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

The best commissioning plan names three people: one for the 3.0 ml verification, one for the encoder handshake, one for the cleaning route. With a 2159–3159 units per hour machine, that is what a week-long delay costs.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The business case for a juice blending and pasteurizing unit is arithmetic before it is technical: 3.0 ml per unit, 2159–3159 units per hour, and a shift count that adds up across a year. Work it that way and the answer is usually within 12–36 months at regional mid-size beverage producers scale.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this juice blending and pasteurizing unit are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this juice blending and pasteurizing unit hold in real production?

The reference build holds 3.0 ml on 500 ml can product at 2159–3159 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Is a 316L stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 500 ml can product the standard grade is not appropriate.

Is the juice blending and pasteurizing unit suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 2159–3159 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
