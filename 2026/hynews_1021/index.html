Medium Speed Filling Line

Published 2026-10-21 · KIWL Machine

A medium speed filling line sits on the critical path of the mid-size beverage producers production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a medium speed filling line.

What This Equipment Does

Quantities that drift by a fraction of a millilitre add up: at 1312 bottles per hour, a 2.0 ml deviation on 60–120 mm diameter containers is measurable give-away every single shift. This medium speed filling line is specified to hold that figure.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference medium speed filling line uses 304/316L stainless steel for product-contact surfaces, a 10-inch colour touch, a 1.5–2.5 kW connected load and a 1800 × 1400 × 2800 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

A medium speed filling line that shares its control protocol with the rest of the mid-size beverage producers production line can be called up by a recipe change. That is what the standard encoder interface on this build is for.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. A medium speed filling line at 1312–1962 bottles per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 60–120 mm diameter containers, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the mid-size beverage producers operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

Build the maintenance plan from the duty cycle rather than the calendar. At 1312–1962 units per hour the medium speed filling line accumulates hours faster than any inspection schedule assumes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the medium speed filling line need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 1400 × 2800 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How are changeovers recorded for traceability?

The 10-inch colour touch stores the recipe per format and the parameters can be exported. For a mid-size beverage producers operation running audits, request the export format in writing.

Can the medium speed filling line be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 1312–1962 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 60–120 mm diameter range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
