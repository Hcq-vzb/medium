Carbonator Unit

Published 2026-10-25 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a carbonator unit for mid-size beverage producers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

The value of a carbonator unit is consistency across shifts. This configuration holds 518–768 units per hour on 330 ml can product, so planning does not have to assume the worst week of the year.

Key Technical Specifications

For a mid-size beverage producers buyer, the specification worth writing into the order is: food-grade 304 stainless steel product-contact parts, 7-inch colour touch controls, 1.5 ml tolerance, 12-minute changeover, 38–53 kW connected load and a 2400 × 1900 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Physical integration is the easy half. The harder half is whether the mid-size beverage producers team will adopt the routine — and a carbonator unit with a 12-minute changeover is far easier to adopt than one that needs a technician.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the carbonator unit on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 330 ml can product with a 12-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

The carbonator unit was designed so the routine work happens during a changeover rather than during production. Seals, sensors and drives are grouped for access, which is what allows a 518 unit per hour mid-size beverage producers production line to maintain without a shutdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this carbonator unit?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 12 minutes. The first change to a new 330 ml can format takes longer.

How is the carbonator unit documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

Can the carbonator unit run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 7-inch colour touch, yes. The mid-size beverage producers team still owns the routine checks, but the 12-minute changeover is guided.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
