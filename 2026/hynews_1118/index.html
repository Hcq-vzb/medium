Empty Bottle Inspector

Published 2026-11-18 · KIWL Machine

Choosing the right empty bottle inspector is a practical decision for mid-size beverage producers. This guide covers the criteria that matter when specifying, integrating and accepting an empty bottle inspector on a production line, with the figures a buyer should confirm in writing before placing an order.

What This Equipment Does

The value of an empty bottle inspector is consistency across shifts. This configuration holds 16655–21155 units per hour on 20 L product, so planning does not have to assume the worst week of the year.

Key Technical Specifications

The three figures that move the price most are accuracy, contact-part grade and the control platform. On this empty bottle inspector the tolerance is 0.3 ml, the contact parts are 304/316L stainless steel, and the controls run on a 4-inch HMI.

Integration With Your Line

An empty bottle inspector that shares its control protocol with the rest of the mid-size beverage producers production line can be called up by a recipe change. That is what the standard encoder interface on this build is for.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Three mechanisms produce the return on an empty bottle inspector: less material lost, fewer hours worked, less time stopped. At a 0.3 ml tolerance and 16655–21155 units per hour, all three move together for a mid-size beverage producers operation.

Maintenance and Service

The empty bottle inspector was specified with wear part lead times in mind, so a mid-size beverage producers operation is not left waiting a week for a seal while the line stands at 16655 units per hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the empty bottle inspector?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 16655 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How is the empty bottle inspector documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

Is the empty bottle inspector suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 16655–21155 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
