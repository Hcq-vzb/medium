Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

What is the delivery lead time for the 8000bph carbonated line?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 12 oz set is included.
