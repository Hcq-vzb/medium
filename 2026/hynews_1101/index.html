Medium Speed Canning Line

Published 2026-11-01 · KIWL Machine

The figures in this guide are the ones a supplier should be held to in writing. For a medium speed canning line in mid-size beverage producers production, they cover rate, tolerance, materials, control, changeover and acceptance.

What This Equipment Does

The value of a medium speed canning line is consistency across shifts. This configuration holds 10520–14020 units per hour on 1.5 L product, so planning does not have to assume the worst week of the year.

Key Technical Specifications

For a medium speed canning line aimed at mid-size beverage producers operations, the practical reference build is 304/316L stainless steel contact parts, a 4-inch HMI and a 27–38 kW connected load inside 1200 × 2200 × 3200 mm.

Integration With Your Line

A medium speed canning line that shares its control protocol with the rest of the mid-size beverage producers production line can be called up by a recipe change. That is what the standard encoder interface on this build is for.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare the medium speed canning line against the cost of the manual step it removes, including the labour during changeover. At 12 minutes per format change and 10520 units per hour, the saving is measurable in shift hours.

Maintenance and Service

Remote support matters for a medium speed canning line because the fastest diagnoses come from the actual machine data: a 4-inch HMI that records the last 12 minutes of a fault tells you more than a phone call.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the medium speed canning line behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the medium speed canning line determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

Can the medium speed canning line be upgraded later?

Within the 10520–14020 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 1.5 L range before ordering.

How is the medium speed canning line documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
