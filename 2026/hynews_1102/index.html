Beverage Pasteurizer Tunnel

Published 2026-11-02 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a beverage pasteurizer tunnel for mid-size beverage producers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

Ask a mid-size beverage producers plant engineer to describe their best-performing beverage pasteurizer tunnel and they will describe the one that runs for weeks without intervention. This configuration is specified at 14603–18603 units per hour on 1.5 L product with that as the design target.

Key Technical Specifications

Every quotation for a beverage pasteurizer tunnel contains a headline rate and a long list of options. The four entries that actually determine the outcome are the 0.5 ml tolerance, the 304 stainless steel contact-part grade, the 1.5–2.5 kW load and the 1800 × 1400 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

The costliest mistake is treating a beverage pasteurizer tunnel as a standalone machine. Once it accepts a line encoder signal and works across a 1800 mm working width, it becomes part of the mid-size beverage producers control loop and recipe changes stop being manual.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. A beverage pasteurizer tunnel at 14603–18603 units per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 1.5 L product, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the mid-size beverage producers operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

Record every intervention against the beverage pasteurizer tunnel. After a few months the pattern of the seals and the drive tells you the real service interval, which is usually shorter than the manual claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the beverage pasteurizer tunnel be upgraded later?

Within the 14603–18603 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 1.5 L range before ordering.

How long does a format changeover take on this beverage pasteurizer tunnel?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 12 minutes. The first change to a new 1.5 L format takes longer.

Can the beverage pasteurizer tunnel run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 7-inch colour touch, yes. The mid-size beverage producers team still owns the routine checks, but the 12-minute changeover is guided.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
