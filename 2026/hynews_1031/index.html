Frequently Asked Questions

Is a 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 330 ml product the standard grade is not appropriate.

What is the actual connected load of the beverage mixer and carbonator?

The reference build draws 8.5–11.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

Is the beverage mixer and carbonator suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 973–1323 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
