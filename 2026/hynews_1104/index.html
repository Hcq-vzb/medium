Beverage Packing Line

Published 2026-11-04 · KIWL Machine

The difference between a workable beverage packing line and an expensive mistake usually shows up six months later, in downtime and in service calls. This guide covers the specification points that decide the outcome for mid-size beverage producers, in the order they are best resolved.

What This Equipment Does

Every machine on a line should have one sentence that explains why it exists. For this beverage packing line it is: it processes 500 ml can product at 18564–23064 units per hour, consistently, without the mid-size beverage producers team chasing it around.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a beverage packing line on the specification that limits your output: the 2.0 ml tolerance, the food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts and the 15-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Confirm the working width of 2400 mm against the actual conveyor pitch, not against a catalogue. That single check removes most of the surprises that appear after a beverage packing line arrives on a mid-size beverage producers floor.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the beverage packing line over five years rather than one budget cycle. food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts, a 2.0 ml tolerance and a 15-minute changeover have different cost curves, and the purchase price tells you very little about any of them.

Maintenance and Service

The beverage packing line keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 18564 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the beverage packing line?

The reference build draws 6.5–8.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

What accuracy can this beverage packing line hold in real production?

The reference build holds 2.0 ml on 500 ml can product at 18564–23064 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

What is the delivery lead time for the beverage packing line?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 500 ml can set is included.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
